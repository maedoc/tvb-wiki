## Abstract

Vision-based measurement techniques are increasingly employed across diverse industrial applications, where image quality serves as a crucial determinant of measurement accuracy. This paper focuses on a 3D expansion measurement system specialized for power plant utility boilers. A composite image quality assessment metric is proposed for this system, and a model is correspondingly built through discrete cosine transform (DCT), with the image quality metric as the output and brightness and exposure time as the inputs. Three image quality control strategies are respectively proposed with consideration of light pollution mitigation and image quality metric enhancement. Through comprehensive simulation experiments, we validate the effectiveness of these control approaches. Comparative analysis demonstrates that strategy 3 emerges as the optimal solution, offering superior performance through effective light pollution suppression while maintaining enhanced image quality metrics.
