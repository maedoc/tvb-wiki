## Abstract

The identification and diagnosis of this brain tumour provide significant challenges in medical imaging, since accuracy, efficiency, and timeliness are crucial for therapy. Traditional methods are inefficient and often susceptible to errors because of their significant dependence on human interpretation of MRI images. To address these shortcomings, we used YOLOv8, a most recent deep learning model, for more precise detection of brain tumors. We trained YOLOv8 on an extensive dataset of MRI pictures, enabling the real-time detection and segmentation of tumours. The model substantially surpassed conventional architectures like as CNN, U-Net, and ResNet 152, with an accuracy rate of $\mathbf{9 9. 4 5 \%}$ and an F1 score of $\mathbf{9 9. 8 2 \%}$. YOLOv8 model demonstrates superior picture processing speed without compromising accuracy, making it a vital tool for clinical applications. This work sets YOLOv8 as a new baseline for brain tumour identification, demonstrating substantial improvements in both speed and accuracy.
