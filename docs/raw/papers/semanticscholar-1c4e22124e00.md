## Abstract

To investigate the generation mechanisms of gamma oscillations during cognitive tasks, this study develops a multi-scale neural computational model that integrates a microscopic excitatory/inhibitory (E/I) neural network with a macroscopic Wilson-Cowan neural mass model. A top-down and bottom-up coupling mechanism is proposed to simulate the modulatory effects of external input differences between excitatory and inhibitory neurons on gamma activity under task conditions. Simulation results demonstrate that the generation of gamma oscillations depends on the asymmetry of E/I inputs, while the macroscopic model exhibits stable responses to perturbations. Further validation with EEG data recorded during a flight task reveals a significant increase in gamma power in the prefrontal cortex and other regions during task performance, consistent with model predictions. This study provides a theoretical basis for modeling cognitive state assessment.
