## Abstract

Process, Voltage and Temperature variations are inherent characteristics of semiconductor manufacturing and operational environments that significantly impact circuit performance, timing and power consumption. These variations can cause substantial deviations from nominal design specifications, making it crucial to account for their effects during both design and training phases of neuromorphic systems. This work presents an approach for implementing Spiking Neural Networks using Leaky Integrate-and-Fire neurons with Process, Voltage and Temperature variation-aware training. We propose a hardwareefficient architecture that utilizes binary weight arrays and processes them through current multiplier circuits before feeding into LIF neurons. The simulations in this work were carried out using Python models and $28 n m$ CMOS process. Our work addresses the critical challenge of maintaining network performance under real-world hardware variations while achieving significant area and hence power savings through optimized quantization schemes. Experimental results demonstrate that our PVT-aware training approach maintains robust classification performance across the XOR evaluation and N-MNIST classification.
