## Abstract

Mobile and edge artificial intelligence (AI) is enabling real-time decoding and generation of brain signals such as EEG and fMRI. However, deploying complex brain-AI models on lightweight devices remains difficult because of limited computation, latency, and interpretability. This paper reviews recent progress in combining real-time statistical inference and explainable generative modeling for brain signal analysis in mobile and edge environments. Probabilistic and variational methods allow adaptive decoding of dynamic neural activity, while edge-aware generative frameworks improve efficiency without losing neuroscientific accuracy. Explainability techniques, including attribution analysis and uncertainty estimation, further enhance transparency and reliability. By integrating advances from neuroscience, machine learning, and edge computing, this work outlines a path toward real-time, interpretable, and energy-efficient brain-AI systems that can operate reliably in everyday applications.
