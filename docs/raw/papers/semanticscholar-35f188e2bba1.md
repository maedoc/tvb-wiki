## Abstract

Understanding the emergence of pathological brain dynamics remains one of the greatest challenges in neuroscience. In this study, we propose a novel dynamical framework that integrates neurons, astrocytes, and synapses into a unified chaotic system. Our model couples a FitzHugh–Nagumo neuron with astrocytic calcium regulation and synaptic transmission mechanisms, using a Rössler attractor to introduce chaos into the inter-component communication. The chaotic coupling simulates abnormal interactions observed in neurodegenerative and neurological disorders such as epilepsy, Alzheimer’s disease, and autism. Simulation results reveal transitions from normal to pathological states through bifurcation and Lyapunov analyses. This multi-scale approach offers a new perspective for understanding how microscopic chaotic perturbations can give rise to macroscopic brain dysfunctions. The proposed model also serves as a computational testbed for exploring therapeutic modulation of glial–neuronal interactions.
