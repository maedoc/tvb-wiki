                                    (WM) injury. Edge density imaging (EDI) is a novel technique that has demonstrated reliability in

                                    intensities of adipose and non-adipose tissue. Scans were processed by a pipeline (MaPPeRTrac) to

                                    increased risk of dementia, with sex as a potential differential factor. EDI demonstrates promise in
                                    delineating the neuropathology of obesity and dementia.
