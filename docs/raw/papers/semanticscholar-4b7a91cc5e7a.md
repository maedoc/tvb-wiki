## Abstract

As Alzheimer's advances, it develops into a degenerative neurological condition which significantly affects brain connectivity and progressively decreases memory and cognitive abilities. For prompt intervention and better patient care, early diagnosis is essential. By employing brain MRI scans in combination with convolutional neural networks (CNN), this study suggests an automated method for recognizing and categorizing the successive phases of Alzheimer's progression. W ith the help of Python & TensorFlow, the model is trained on four types of MRI images: Normal cognition, very mild dementia stage, mild dementia stage, and moderate dementia stage. To improve the effectiveness of the algorithm, the approach utilizes preprocessing methods including over-sampling and augmentation. To facilitate real-time categorization through straightforward MRI picture uploads, an intuitive Flask-based web application was also created. Primary performance metrics were employed to assess the model, and for the nondemented class, an AUC of 0.94 was recorded for the model, with corresponding F1-score, precision, and recall values of 0.90, 0.89, and 0.92 respectively. The F1- scores for the remaining classes are obtained between 0.69 and 0.79, suggesting consistent performance in closely related categories with room for improvement. This work demonstrates the promise of AI in improving early-stage Alzheimer's detection and acts as a proof-of-concept for incorporating deep learning into healthcare diagnostics.
