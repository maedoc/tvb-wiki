## Abstract

Diagnosing Autism Spectrum Disorder (ASD) presents a significant challenge due to its inherent heterogeneity and reliance on time-consuming clinical assessments. Traditional methods can be subjective and may lead to diagnostic delays, which are particularly detrimental given the importance of early intervention. To address these limitations, a growing body of research is exploring the use of neuroimaging-based diagnostics combined with machine learning (ML) models. This paper provides a comprehensive review of the current state of this research, comparing and contrasting various algorithms used for ASD classification. Our review process systematically examines a wide range of studies, focusing on the diversity of ML techniques from classical models to advanced deep learning architectures and the different neuroimaging modalities employed, such as functional and structural MRI. We analyze how different data preprocessing pipelines and feature extraction methods influence model performance and generalizability. Furthermore, we provide our own interpretations of the findings, discuss common challenges such as sample heterogeneity and data scarcity, and outline future directions for developing more robust, reproducible, and clinically applicable diagnostic tools. This work aims to synthesize the collective knowledge in the field and guide future research toward creating reliable, automated solutions that can augment clinical practice.
