## Abstract

Proper segmentation of medical images helps greatly in identifying diseases, creating treatment plans, and doing clinical studies. Traditional CNNs perform well, though they usually have difficulties capturing both detailed and general information in medical images with various anatomies. We are proposing a new Multi-Scale Graph Convolutional Network (MS-GCN) in this paper as it allows mutli-scale feature learning and benefits from graph-based understanding to improve segmentation performance. Within the MS-GCN structure, ResNet-34 helps in feature hierarchy and graph convolutional layers are used to connect different parts of anatomical structures. Trying our model on BraTS 2020, Synapse Multi-Organ CT, and MoNuSeg data, we observed that it achieves greater accuracy than U-Net, ResUNet, DenseNet, and conventional GCN models on the Dice Similarity Coefficient. According to the findings, the proposed MS-GCN has enhanced results in outlining the brain’s boundaries, coping with individual differences, and performing well on different types of images. These findings prove that MS-GCN is suitable and can be applied to different medical image segmentation challenges.
