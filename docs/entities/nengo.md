# Nengo

**Nengo** is a Python-based toolkit for building and simulating large-scale neural models. It is particularly known for the Neural Engineering Framework (NEF), which allows construction of neural networks that compute mathematical functions.

## Relationship to TVB

Nengo operates at a different scale from TVB but shares theoretical foundations in population-level neural dynamics. Both frameworks use rate-based population coding, and Nengo's NEF principles inform the mathematical formulation of TVB neural mass models.
