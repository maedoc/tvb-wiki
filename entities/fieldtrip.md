FieldTrip (fieldtrip) is a open-source MATLAB toolbox for advanced analysis of MEG, EEG, and invasive electrophysiological data, specializing in source reconstruction and [[connectivity]].

## Key Features

* Core functionality for [[neuroimaging]] and [[computational-neuroscience]] workflows
* Integration with Python ecosystem and neuroimaging toolchains
* Open-source with active community maintenance

## Relationship to Whole-Brain Modeling

FieldTrip is often used alongside [[tvb]] and other simulation platforms in pre-processing or post-processing pipelines for [[connectome]]-based brain modeling.
