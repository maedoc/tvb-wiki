FSLeyes (fsleyes) is a interactive viewer for 3D and 4D [[neuroimaging]] data, part of the FSL suite, supporting overlays, atlases, and statistical maps.

## Key Features

* Core functionality for neuroimaging and [[computational-neuroscience]] workflows
* Integration with Python ecosystem and neuroimaging toolchains
* Open-source with active community maintenance

## Relationship to Whole-Brain Modeling

FSLeyes is often used alongside [[tvb]] and other simulation platforms in pre-processing or post-processing pipelines for [[connectome]]-based brain modeling.
