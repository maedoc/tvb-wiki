DIPY (dipy) is a Python library for [[diffusion-mri]] analysis, including reconstruction, fiber tracking, and statistical analysis of diffusion data.

## Key Features

* Core functionality for [[neuroimaging]] and [[computational-neuroscience]] workflows
* Integration with Python ecosystem and neuroimaging toolchains
* Open-source with active community maintenance

## Relationship to Whole-Brain Modeling

DIPY is often used alongside [[tvb]] and other simulation platforms in pre-processing or post-processing pipelines for [[connectome]]-based brain modeling.
