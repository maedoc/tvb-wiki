SPM (spm) is a Statistical Parametric Mapping — MATLAB-based software suite for the analysis of brain imaging data sequences, especially [[fmri]], PET, and VBM.

## Key Features

* Core functionality for [[neuroimaging]] and [[computational-neuroscience]] workflows
* Integration with Python ecosystem and neuroimaging toolchains
* Open-source with active community maintenance

## Relationship to Whole-Brain Modeling

SPM is often used alongside [[tvb]] and other simulation platforms in pre-processing or post-processing pipelines for [[connectome]]-based brain modeling.
