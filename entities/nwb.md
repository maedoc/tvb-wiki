[[neurodata-without-borders]] (NWB) (nwb) is a standardized data format for neurophysiology data, designed to promote data sharing and [[reproducibility]] across laboratories.

## Key Features

* Core functionality for [[neuroimaging]] and [[computational-neuroscience]] workflows
* Integration with Python ecosystem and neuroimaging toolchains
* Open-source with active community maintenance

## Relationship to Whole-Brain Modeling

Neurodata Without Borders (NWB) is often used alongside [[tvb]] and other simulation platforms in pre-processing or post-processing pipelines for [[connectome]]-based brain modeling.
