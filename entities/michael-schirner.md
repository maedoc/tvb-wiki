# Michael Schirner

Researcher in personalized brain modeling and TVB pipeline development.

## Research Focus

- Automated pipelines for personalized virtual brain construction
- Integration of [[neuroimaging]] data into whole-brain models
- TVB workflow development and validation

## Key Publication

- Schirner et al. (2018) — Automated pipeline for personalized virtual brains schirner-2018
  - Automated end-to-end pipeline from MRI/DWI to TVB models
  - [[parcellation]], [[tractography]], and [[connectivity]] estimation
  - Validation across multiple datasets
  - Lowered barrier for large cohort studies
