## Definition
Nonlinear dynamics studies systems where output is not proportional to input, giving rise to complex behaviors including oscillations, multistability, chaos, and pattern formation that cannot occur in [[linear]] systems.

## Characteristics of Nonlinear Systems

### Superposition Failure
- Response to combined inputs ≠ sum of individual responses
- Interactions between components are essential
- Cannot decompose into independent modes

### Rich Phenomenology
- Multiple stable states (multistability)
- Self-sustained oscillations
- Chaos and sensitive dependence
- Pattern formation and waves

### Nonlinear Phenomena
- Saturation and threshold effects
- Hysteresis
- Frequency entrainment
- Mode locking

## In Neuroscience

### Neural Models
- [[neuron]] threshold and saturation
- [[neural mass model]] nonlinearities
- Coupled population dynamics
- brain oscillations emergence

### Whole-Brain Modeling
- [[whole brain]] simulations are inherently nonlinear
- Network effects amplify nonlinearities
- [[structural connectivity]] constrains dynamics
- [[functional connectivity]] emerges from nonlinear interactions

## Mathematical Tools
- Phase plane analysis
- Nullclines and isoclines
- Poincaré maps
- Lyapunov exponents

## Related Concepts
- [[dynamical systems theory]] — General framework
- [[bifurcation theory]] — Parameter-induced changes
- brain oscillations — Nonlinear oscillations
- [[mean field theory]] — Approximate analysis

## References
- strogatz-1994 — Comprehensive introduction
- wiggins-2003 — Geometric methods
- ermentrout-terman-2010 — Neuroscience applications

## Summary
The seminal textbook on nonlinear dynamics and chaos theory, providing accessible introduction to bifurcation theory, phase portraits, limit cycles, and strange attractors. Widely used in computational neuroscience for understanding neural mass model dynamics.

## Key Concepts
- Phase plane analysis and nullclines
- Bifurcation types (saddle-node, transcritical, pitchfork, Hopf)
- Limit cycles and Poincaré-Bendixson theorem
- Chaos and the Lorenz system
- Applications to biological oscillators

## Relevance to TVB
- Foundation for understanding [[neural mass model]] bifurcations
- Used to analyze [[epilepsy modeling]] seizure transitions
- Underlies [[bifurcation analysis]] in whole-brain simulations
