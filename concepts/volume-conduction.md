title: Volume Conduction
type: concept
updated: '2026-05-06'
---

Volume conduction refers to the passive, instantaneous spread of electrical current through biological tissue without signal propagation or delay. In [[neuroimaging]] and [[computational-neuroscience]], it is the physical process by which primary currents generated by neuronal activity—primarily from postsynaptic potentials in pyramidal cells—propagate through the head volume and manifest as measurable potentials on the scalp (EEG) or magnetic fields outside the head (MEG). Unlike propagation along axons, volume conduction involves no neural transmission delay
