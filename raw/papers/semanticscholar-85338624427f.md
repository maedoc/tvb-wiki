## Abstract

Clinical workflow for symptom-based brain tumour detection for a patient involves medical imaging through Magnetic Resonance Imaging (MRI), processing and analysing the image through AI-assisted tools, followed by manual observation by a radiologist. Even with extensive research and technology integration, the existing methodologies do not provide exact tumour area highlighting and pixelated area estimation with accurate classification of tumour type, and thus fall short of complete automation. This paper contributes to this research gap by providing a neural network with a YOLOv8-DeepLab and EfficientNet embedded into a pipeline, which provides efficient four-class tumour classification, their location and area highlighting with size evaluation. The proposed model achieves an accuracy of 96.2 %, thus ensuring a state-of-the-art method performance along with additional tumour analysis features, thus carrying merit for better clinical adoption for treatment planning and monitoring.
