## Abstract

In this paper, an automatic analysis method of multivariate econometric model based on AI algorithm is proposed to solve the limitations of traditional linear econometric model in extreme event prediction and high-dimensional data processing. This method breaks through the limitations of traditional models, constructs an adaptive and nonparametric general framework, and realizes the whole process automation by integrating deep learning, reinforcement learning (RL) and Bayesian statistics. Among them, RL's sequential decision-making process is used to simulate the economist's variable screening logic, and the optimal subset is selected from the massive variables; Construct a deep neural network model with multi-head attention mechanism for the selected core variables to enhance interpretability and capture the time-varying importance of variables; Approximate Bayesian inference combined with Monte Carlo Dropout provides reliable confidence intervals and supports robust policy simulation. Empirical research takes the impact of the Federal Reserve's federal funds rate hike on the US real GDP growth rate and CPI inflation rate as an example. The results show that this method can effectively identify key variables, and the out of sample prediction accuracy is significantly better than traditional Bayesian vector autoregression (BVAR) models and LASSO models. It can also clearly demonstrate the dynamic transmission mechanism and prediction uncertainty of policy shocks. This automated analysis framework minimizes subjective human intervention and provides strong support for economic analysis and decision-making.
