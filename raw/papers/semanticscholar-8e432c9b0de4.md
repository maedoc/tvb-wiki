## Abstract

EMI filters are commonly used to solve the electromagnetic interference problem of power electronic converters. But due to the diversity of topologies and the existence of parasitic parameters, it is difficult to accurately model and design an EMI filter using traditional methods. Therefore, it is necessary to investigate an intelligent method to achieve accurate modeling and performance optimization design of EMI filter. A two-step method for EMI filter insertion loss modeling and performance optimization is proposed in this paper. EMI filter insertion loss curve fitting is based on back propagation neural network (BPNN), while optimal filter topology and parameters are selected for performance optimization by using differential evolution (DE) algorithm. Simulations are made for validation and prove that the proposed method has good modeling accuracy and optimization effectiveness.
