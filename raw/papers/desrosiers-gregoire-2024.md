Nature Communications article introducing RABIES (Rodent Automated Bold Improvement of EPI Sequences), an open-source pipeline for rodent fMRI preprocessing, confound correction, analysis, and quality control. Validates preprocessing across 23 multi-site datasets and proposes QC guidelines for network analysis.
