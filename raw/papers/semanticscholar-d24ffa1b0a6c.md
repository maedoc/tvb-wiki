## Abstract

The environment of activity in the autonomous mode in varying conditions is complicated due to unforeseen obstacles and the need to make immediate decisions. The research paper introduces a work that incorporates the use of Spiking Neural Networks (SNNs) and Deep Q-Learning (DQN) to improve the performance of navigating. The sensor inputs that SNN module processes are event driven to reduce the computation needs plus the DQN module is based on reinforcement learning that is able to take better decisions. According to experimental experiments, the developed model achieves 97.2 percent of the static precision of navigation and 88.1 percent of the dynamic precision that is higher than A- and Dijkstra performance. The model also maximizes the time taken in decision-making to 95 ms and reduces the energy use to 31.5J. Apparently, SNN-DQN as an approach to autonomous navigation with real-time and efficient navigation is promising based on its output data.
