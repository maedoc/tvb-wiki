# Mindboggling morphometry of human brains

Klein et al. (2017) describe Mindboggle as an open-source brain morphometry platform that takes preprocessed T1-weighted MRI data and outputs volumetric, surface-based, and tabular data containing anatomical labels, cortical features, and shape measures. The paper evaluates the software across 101 manually labeled brains from the Mindboggle-101 dataset.
