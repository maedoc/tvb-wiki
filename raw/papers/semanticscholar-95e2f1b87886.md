## Abstract

The detection and diagnosis of brain diseases including Alzheimer’s and additional neurodegenerative disorders proves highly challenging to healthcare providers. The current diagnostic approaches depend heavily on healthcare evaluations merged with neuroimaging procedures that require substantial time along with substantial resources. A research investigation introduces a brain disease prediction framework using deep learning algorithms from artificial intelligence. The DenseNet121 model trained from convolutional neural networks operates on MRI scanning data to achieve effective classifications of neurological condition stages. The obtained dataset is acquired from open medical imaging repositories while preprocessing operations strengthen the extraction of features. The model achieves performance evaluation through metrics consisting of accuracy, precision, recall and F1-score. The development of a Flask-Based web application solves the accessibility challenge by delivering live prediction services to healthcare workers. Deep learning systems demonstrate superior capabilities in detecting diseases at early stages thus providing potential benefits to patient health results and medical treatments. The work also demonstrates how artificial intelligence revolutionizes medical diagnostics while establishing a necessity for better AI healthcare solutions development.
