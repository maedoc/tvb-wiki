## Abstract

EEG-based Recognition of Epileptic Seizure patterns involves analyzing brainwave signals to detect abnormal activity indicative of epileptic seizures. Epileptic seizures are difficult to diagnose due to their unpredictability and the complexity of brain signals. Traditional EEG-based detection methods often lack accuracy, efficiency, and real-time performance. To overcome existing challenges in EEG-based epileptic seizure recognition, this study suggests a new framework: A-Lightweight Spiking Convolutional Neural Network with Human Memory Optimization Algorithm (A-lightWSCNNet+HMOA). The framework is designed for efficient and highly accurate identification of seizure patterns. The input EEG data from the CHB-MIT dataset is first preprocessed using Color Wiener Filtering (CWF) to improve the signals’ essential characteristics. Feature extraction is then performed using the Spike-Driven Transformer (SDT), which effectively captures the essential temporal and spatial characteristics of the EEG signals. For classification, the ALightweight Spiking Convolutional Neural Network (A-lightWSCNNet) is employed. Its performance is further optimized using the Human Memory Optimization Algorithm (HMOA), ensuring robust and precise detection of epileptic events. The suggested framework is implemented in Python. The suggested A-lightWSCNNet+HMOA outperforms current approaches with an accuracy of $99.9 \%$ as well as a specificity of $99.8 \%$ when tested on the CHB-MIT dataset. The outcomes of the proposed framework demonstrate superior detection of epileptic seizures with enhanced accuracy, specificity, and robustness, effectively outperforming existing methods in recognizing seizure patterns from EEG signals.
