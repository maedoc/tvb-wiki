## Abstract

The rapid and continuous change in artificial intelligence (AI), Machine Learning (ML) and Deep Learning (DL) along with cyber-physical systems (CPS) incorporating IOT devices have shown the path for transformative innovations in healthcare, particularly in medical imaging. This research work explores the convergence of DL techniques and CPS to enhance the analysis of brain computed tomography (CT) scans. By integrating DL models like Convolutional Neural Networks (CNNs) with CPS that involve data acquisition, processing and decision-making in real-time, this research aims to revolutionize the workflow of medical image analysis. Through the application of DL models like customized CNN, InceptionV3 and Xception, this work demonstrates the potential for automatic and highly accurate detection of brain anomalies, directly from CT scan images obtained from CPS. These models can learn complex patterns in image data, thus significantly reducing human error and improving diagnostic efficiency. Additionally, the integration of cyber-physical systems ensures seamless communication between imaging devices, computational resources and healthcare practitioners, enabling real-time diagnostics, remote monitoring and decision support. Furthermore, this research discusses the potential, limitation and future perspective of the current approach in advancing the intelligent healthcare ecosystem by making automated image analysis accessible, scalable and accurate, ultimately contributing to precision medicine.
