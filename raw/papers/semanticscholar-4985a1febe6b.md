## Abstract

Flying Ad Hoc Networks (FANETs) are gradually used for search-and-rescue, surveillance, and environmental monitoring, but they face serious challenges in energy management and dynamic mobility handling. This paper proposes ECO-SNNRFD, an Energy-Conscious Spiking Neural Network combined with River Formation Dynamics framework, designed to optimize routing efficiency while minimizing energy consumption in UAV-based FANETs. The framework leverages spiking neural networks to predict optimal routes and incorporates river formation dynamics to dynamically adjust routing paths according to network conditions. Extensive MATLAB simulations model a three-dimensional FANET scenario with varying UAV mobility and energy constraints. ECO-SNNRFD is evaluated against two baseline protocols, LFERS [5] and RFDMRP [15], using thirteen performance metrics which include Average Residual Energy, Packet Delivery Ratio, End-to-End Delay, Convergence Time, Load Balancing Index, Network Lifetime, Link Reliability, Energy per Packet, Routing Overhead, Throughput, Path Success Probability, Jitter, and Path Optimality. Results validate that ECO-SNNRFD consistently attains lower delays, higher delivery rates, better energy utilization, and more reliable routing compared to the baselines, confirming its suitability for dynamic FANET environments. The proposed framework provides a energy-efficient, robust solution for practical UAV network deployments.
