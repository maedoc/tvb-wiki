## Abstract

A method for obtaining a spiking artificial neural network (SNN) by converting it from a formal artificial neural network (ANN), using a developed algorithm as an example, is considered. Additionally, the inference of the SNN on memristors is demonstrated. The key advantages of an analog implementation of ANN based on memristors are highlighted. The challenges associated with implementing SNN on memristors are discussed. A comparison of the simulation results of the SNN operation and its inference on memristors is presented.
