## Abstract

Deep learning models have aided in streamlining the segmentation process of Magnetic Resonance Imaging (MRI) scans. Still, the variability of brain tumors presents challenges and underscores the need for advanced methods. 3D U-Net, a variant of U-Net, skips the connections that link the encoder and decoder, preserving spatial details, and is widely used in medical image segmentation. In this study, an advanced 3D U-Net (3D U-Net++), a variant of 3D U-Net that incorporates nested and densely connected skip layers, is proposed. These connections, with intermediate convolutional layers, enable refined feature fusion and better multi-scale information transfer, improving brain tumor segmentation performance. Furthermore, the Encoder-Decoder Convolutional Neural Network (ED-CNN) model is implemented for comparison. The implementations are conducted on MRI images of the BraTS 2018 dataset with four classes. The evaluation metrics considered are accuracy, precision, recall, $\mathbf{F 1}$-score, and Dice Coefficient. The results indicate that 3D U-Net++ achieved an accuracy of 0.9913, precision of 0.9868, recall of 0.9911, F1-score of 0.9879, and Dice Coefficient of 0.9997. In contrast, ED-CNN recorded an accuracy of 0.9937, precision of 0.9876, recall of 0.9937, F1-score of 0.9924, and Dice Coefficient of 0.9999, indicating superior performance.
