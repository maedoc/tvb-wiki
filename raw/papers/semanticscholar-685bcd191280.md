## Abstract

It is important to detect dementia, especially Alzheimer disease much earlier to make useful interventions and care planning. Conventionally, diagnostic tests either include neuroimaging, where tests may be lengthy in nature, or involve clinical interview that is time consuming and subjective. The work presented in this research was aimed at proposing a fuzzy logic-based model using MATLAB that would help in the prediction of the early onset of dementia using both cognitive tests scores and electroencephalogram signal features. Fuzzy Inference System (FIS) is intended to deal with ambiguities and smooth interactions that are characterized by dementia development. Inputs consist of the EEG based variables, alpha/theta ratios, and cognitive assessment scores, provided by instruments, such as Mini- Mental State Examination (MMSE). The model provides the risk dementia score, which is low, moderate or high. The simulation indicates that the fuzzy model correlates with the clinical assessment and is a quick, comprehensible, and affordable diagnostic assistance.
