                                                                                  Our main contributions are summarized as follows:
                                                                                  • We propose LI-DSN, a novel layer-wise interactive dual-
                                                                                    stream network that enables progressive spatiotemporal
                                                                                    integration through continuous interaction, effectively ad-
                                                                                    dressing the “information silo” problem inherent in late-
                                                                                    fusion approaches.
                                                                                  • We design the TSIA mechanism to capture synergis-
                                                                                    tic spatiotemporal representations by integrating spatial
                                                                                    structural priors with temporal dynamics. To augment
                                                                                    these representations, an adaptive fusion strategy is em-
                                                                                    ployed to selectively emphasize critical features through
                                                                                    learnable weights.
                                                                                  • Comprehensive experiments on eight EEG datasets, span-
                                                                                    ning MI classification, emotion recognition, and SSVEP,
                                                                                    consistently demonstrate LI-DSN’s superior performance
                                                                                    over 13 SOTA baselines.
