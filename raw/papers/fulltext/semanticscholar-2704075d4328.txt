                         GRAPHICAL ABSTRACT




                         PUBLIC SUMMARY
                         - This study presents the most reﬁned chimpanzee brain atlas to date, the Chimpanzee Brainnetome Atlas (ChimpBNA).


                         - Chimpanzee-human connectivity divergence deviates from the pattern of cortical expansion.


                         - Species-speciﬁc connectional asymmetric patterns were examined.


                         - Genes related to divergent connectivities suggested inﬂuences on neural circuit development and evolution.


                         - The standardized atlasing approach provides a meaningful method for cross-species comparisons.
