                  S9. A LGORITHMS FOR D EFINING A T EMPLATE FOR P OPULATION - BASED R EGISTRATION
1) Center of {[qj ]}
   To find a template, the intuitive approach would be to take the cross-sectional mean of the functions fj ∈ F. However, this
would be a poor estimator of the mean because we do not have a distance in C that is invariant to warping. Instead, we can
take the mean of the orbits {[qj ]} which live in the the quotient space S = S∞ /Γ. We have already defined a distance between
elements q1 , q2 ∈ S∞ in section II-A. Since the group Γ acts isometrically on S∞ , under appropriate regularity conditions the
distance defined on S∞ descends via the quotient map to induce a well-defined Riemannian structure on the quotient space S.
As such, we can define the Karcher mean [2] of points in S as

2) Center of the Orbit [µ]

Definition S9.1. For a given set of functions q1 , q2 , ..., qN ∈ Q, and orbit, [µ] ∈ S, define the element µ∗ as the center of [µ]
with respect to the set {qj } if the warping functions γj ∈ Γ, where γj = arg minγ∈Γ ∥µ∗ − (qj ∗ γ)∥2 , have the Karcher mean
γid .
The algorithm to find the center of the orbit is presented in [3], but we will state it here in a more general form.

Algorithm 2 The center of a orbit [µ] w.r.t. the set {qj }
  1) For each qj ∈ Q, find the warping functions γj ∈ Γ by solving γj = arg minγ∈Γ ∥µ − (qj ∗ γ)∥2 using Algorithm 2 in
     the main text.
  2) Compute the Karcher mean γ̄p of all γj using Algorithm (1), where the exponential and logarithmic maps are on Γ
     instead of S, and we initialise the algorithm using γid .
  3) The center of [µ] w.r.t. {qj } is given by µ∗ = (µk ∗ γ̄p−1 ), where µk is the output from Algorithm 2 in the main text.

Algorithm 3 Registration of ConCon Functions
  1) Given functions f1 , f2 , . . . , fN ∈ F, compute q1 , q2 , . . . , qN ∈ Q using the Q map (Equation (3) in the main text).
  2) Compute the Karcher mean (or median) orbit [µ] of [q1 ], [q2 ], . . . , [qN ] ∈ S using Algorithm (1).
