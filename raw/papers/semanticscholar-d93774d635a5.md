## Abstract

This paper presents an intelligent Electronic Throttle Control (ETC) system for Drive-by-Wire automotive platforms that replaces mechanical throttle linkages with a Neural Network-based controller capable of handling nonlinear throttle dynamics including stiction, hysteresis, and spring-back effects. The system incorporates real-time switching between Sport and Eco driving modes, with Eco mode limiting throttle commands for energy efficiency and Sport mode providing unrestricted response for highperformance driving. Developed in MATLAB/Simulink with the Neural Network trained on PID reference data, simulation results demonstrate superior NN performance over conventional PID controllers in rise time, overshoot, stability, and response smoothness, with an embedded-ready architecture enabling real-time ECU deployment for future electric and autonomous vehicles.
