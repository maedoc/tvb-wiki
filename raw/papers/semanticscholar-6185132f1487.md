## Abstract

The fusion of quantum principles with semiconductor bio interfaces marks a paradigm shift in biological system modeling at the edge. This paper introduces a quantum-inspired framework for simulating action potentials in excitable cells by integrating two-level quantum systems with classical Hodgkin-Huxley dynamics. Leveraging Python-optimized tools such as QuTiP, SciPy, and TensorFlow Lite, we execute real-time neural emulation on low-power edge processors. The system architecture incorporates semiconductor-ion channel interfaces modeled as Field Effect Transistors (FETs), translating ionic fluxes into electrical signals with remarkable energy efficiency. Preliminary benchmarks reveal a significant power reduction over classical neuron simulators. Applications span from implantable neuroprosthetics to smart biosensors and artificial intelligence of things (AIoT) biohybrids. Our results lay the groundwork for next-generation bio intelligent systems-combining quantum-inspired computation, embedded AI, and synthetic biology at the edge.
