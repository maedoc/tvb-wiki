## Abstract

Spike-driven information processing is essential for the high parallelism and energy efficiency of neuromorphic computing systems. The Leaky Integrate-and-Fire (LIF) neuron model is popular in digital hardware implementations because it is easy to understand mathematically and can be used for real-time computation. In this study, a discrete-time LIF neuron intended for FPGA-based spiking neural network acceleration was realized. In the proposed a fixed-point numerical representation to perform threshold comparison, weighted spike aggregation, and controlled membrane decay, reset while minimizing logic consumption. Clock-synchronous architecture is made possible by deterministic spike timing and scalable network integration in which the neuron dynamics are implemented. The results of the synthesis were verified by a stable operating frequency and effective resource utilization, and simulation of time-varying spike inputs was used to perform functional validation. In the proposed design, a modular building block was established for constructing large-scale neuromorphic processors aimed at edge-intelligent systems and real-time pattern recognition.
