## Abstract

This study presents a modular neuroadaptive VR framework that uses simulated neural network dynamics to support therapeutic applications across diverse populations. Built on the XyberSync platform, the system adapts in real time to users' cognitive and emotional states, enhancing engagement and personalization. Two key modules demonstrate its versatility: Skyward Sojourn, which offers exposure therapy for acrophobia through immersive virtual environments, and a play therapy module for children with autism, designed to promote emotional regulation and social interaction. Early testing shows improved user engagement and therapeutic response, supporting the framework’s potential for scalable, brain-inspired digital interventions.
Research Objective: The objectives of this research are to develop a modular virtual reality framework that leverages neural network simulations for adaptive therapeutic environments, create and evaluate VR modules for acrophobia exposure therapy and autism play therapy, assess the impact of neuroadaptive feedback on user engagement and therapeutic outcomes, and demonstrate the framework’s flexibility across diverse neurotherapeutic applications.
