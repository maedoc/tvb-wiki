## Abstract

This paper introduces the multimodal system of emotion recognition EmotiFusionX, which integrates Electroencephalography (EEG) signals with emotions of the DEAP dataset obtained using the algorithm Open face. EmotiFusion X uses a combination of Graph Neural Networks (GNNs) to model dynamic EEG connectivity patterns and self-supervised Vision Transformers (ViTs) to study high-resolution facial expression with neural models that can interact using a novel Cross-Modal Explainable Attention (CMEA) mechanism. Innovative techniques proposed in this system lie in four areas: (1) comprehensive preprocessing techniques, including adaptive Independent Component Analysis(ICA) and tunable Q- factor wavelet transforms to remove EEG artifacts and optical-flow-based micro-expression tracking and diffusion-model-based facial occlusion recovery; (2) transparent intermodal bridging via quantifiable attention weights in the CMEA, along with the Layer-wise Relevance Propagation (LRP) that localizes decisive features in the modalities; (3) state-of-the-art, with classification performance of 91.2% arousal classification accuracy and 93.8% valence accuracy. These findings provide novel standards in terms of interpretability at the expense of high accuracy levels, therefore bridging the critical performance explainability gap in affective computing, where fields of application may run the spectrum between diagnosis of mental health and adaptive human-computer interfaces
