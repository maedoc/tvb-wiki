## Abstract

Our aim in this study is to develop a model that predicts an individual's age accurately based on their brain structure and functional changes captured through a T1- weighted MRI automated scan. This robust model will independently learn spatial features of the MRI data without needing human supervision by utilizing several distinct algorithms such as Dense-Net, LSTM, and triplet networks. The trained model learns to predict the brain age. The model assesses volumetric changes in gray matter, white matter, and cortical tissue in addition to the subject's overall structural brain composition. Our project strives to address the needs of people of various ages who have differing brain structural complexities.
