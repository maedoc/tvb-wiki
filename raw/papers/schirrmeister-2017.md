## Abstract

We introduce a new deep learning toolbox (called "braindecode") for decoding from electroneurographic (EEG) data and demonstrate its usefulness by obtaining highly competitive results on multiple subjects on two benchmark datasets: (1) the binary image perception based classifier and (2) the 2013 Brain-Computer Interface Competition. The toolbox supports a flexible and extensible library of convolutional neural network (CNN) architectures as well as a range of visualization options for network activation patterns, including plots of temporal and spatial CNN filter weights, feature activation, and Saliency maps. The code is publicly available to facilitate replicability and encourage future development by the community.
