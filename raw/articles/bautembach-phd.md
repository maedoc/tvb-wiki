# Dennis Bautembach PhD Research

**Author**: Dennis Bautembach
**URL**: https://bautembach.de
**Institution**: University of Crete
**Period**: 2015–2021

## Summary

Dennis Bautembach conducted doctoral research on high-performance spiking neural network simulation on GPGPUs and multi-GPU parallelization. Key contributions include the Spice simulator and several optimization techniques.

## Key Publications

- **PhD Thesis**: "Simulating SNNs on Multiple GPUs - When a Microsecond is an Eternity" (2021). Unpublished doctoral dissertation summarizing research on parallel SNN simulation.

- **HPEC 2021**: "Even Faster SNN Simulation with Lazy+Event-driven Plasticity and Shared Atomics". Introduced shared memory-based spike delivery and lazy event-driven plasticity, speeding up Spice by more than 2x.

- **IJCNN 2021**: "Multi-GPU SNN Simulation with Static Load Balancing". First SNN simulator to scale to 8 GPUs.

- **IJCNN 2020**: "Faster and Simpler SNN Simulation with Work Queues". Spice achieves up to 3x faster simulation than competing simulators through work-queue-based spike propagation and a modern C++ API.
