# ABIDE Dataset

**Full name**: Autism Brain Imaging Data Exchange

**URL**: http://fcon_1000.projects.nitrc.org/indi/abide/

**Primary publication**: Di Martino A, et al. (2014). The autism brain imaging data exchange: towards a large-scale assessment of the core and atypical autism spectrum. *Molecular Autism*. 5:47.

**Secondary publication**: Di Martino A, et al. (2017). Enhancing studies on the autism brain imaging data exchange (ABIDE II). *Biological Psychiatry*. 81:S244.

## Dataset Overview

ABIDE is a multi-site consortium dataset aggregating neuroimaging data from individuals with autism spectrum disorder (ASD) and matched neurotypical controls. Released in two phases (ABIDE I, 2012; ABIDE II, 2017), the dataset includes preprocessed and raw resting-state fMRI data from approximately 20 sites worldwide.

## Key Characteristics

- **Sample size**: >1,000 individuals with ASD and matched controls
- **Imaging modalities**: T1-weighted structural MRI, resting-state fMRI
- **Phenotypic data**: Age, sex, IQ, ADOS, ADI-R scores
- **Preprocessing**: C-PAC pipeline, AFNI, fmriprep variants
